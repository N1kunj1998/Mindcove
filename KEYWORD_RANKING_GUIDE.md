# Keyword Ranking Guide for Your Website

## How Keyword Ranking Works

### 1. **Search Engine Process**
- **Crawling**: Google bots visit your website and read all content
- **Indexing**: Google stores your pages in its database
- **Ranking**: When someone searches, Google matches keywords and ranks pages by relevance and authority
- **Display**: Top-ranked pages appear first in search results

### 2. **Ranking Factors**
- ✅ **Relevance**: How well your content matches the search query
- ✅ **Quality**: Content depth, uniqueness, helpfulness
- ✅ **Authority**: Backlinks, domain age, trust signals
- ✅ **User Experience**: Page speed, mobile-friendliness, bounce rate
- ✅ **Technical SEO**: Meta tags, structured data, sitemap (you've done this!)
- ✅ **Freshness**: Recent updates and new content

## Monitoring Your Rankings

### **Set Up Google Search Console** (Essential!)
1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Add your property (mindcove.io)
3. Verify ownership
4. Submit sitemap.xml
5. Monitor:
   - Which keywords you rank for
   - Your average position
   - Click-through rates
   - Impressions

### **Track These Metrics**
- **Impressions**: How many times your site appears in search
- **Clicks**: How many people click through
- **Average Position**: Your ranking (lower is better - 1 is #1)
- **CTR**: Click-through rate (clicks/impressions)
