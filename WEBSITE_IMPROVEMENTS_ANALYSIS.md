## 🎯 HIGH PRIORITY IMPROVEMENTS

### 1. **Missing FAQ Section** ⚠️ CRITICAL
**Current State**: No FAQ section exists
**Impact**: High - Users have questions, FAQ builds trust, improves SEO
**Recommendation**: Add comprehensive FAQ section with:
- "What are ATS-optimized resume templates?"
- "How do I use the STAR method?"
- "Is there a money-back guarantee?"
- "How quickly will I receive the product?"
- "Can I use these templates for any industry?"
- "What format will I receive the files in?"

**SEO Benefit**: FAQ schema markup can show rich snippets in search results

### 2. **Missing Social Proof Elements** ⚠️ HIGH

### 3. **No Navigation/Header** ⚠️ HIGH
**Current State**: No navigation menu or header

- Navigation links (Features, Testimonials, Pricing, FAQ)

- Mobile hamburger menu

### 4. **Missing Content Depth** ⚠️ HIGH
**Current State**: Single page with limited content

### 5. **Weak Call-to-Action Strategy** ⚠️ MEDIUM-HIGH
**Current State**: CTAs are present but could be optimized

**Recommendations**:
